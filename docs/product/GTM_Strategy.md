# Go-to-market strategy

## Positioning

For cross-functional launch leads at B2B SaaS companies, Launch Control is a readiness and decision workspace that turns launch policy into visible, enforceable gates.

## Initial audience

- Primary: product operations, release managers, and product leads coordinating multi-team launches.
- Secondary: engineering, customer success, security, and communications owners who supply readiness evidence.

## Pilot motion

Run a time-boxed pilot with three launch teams. Model one upcoming launch per team, observe the final readiness review, and compare decision reconstruction time with the existing process. Keep all production actions outside the product during validation.

## Proposed message

“Know who is ready, what proves it, and who made the call.” Lead with enforced gates and a durable decision trail.

## Proposed launch gates

- At least 85% unassisted completion in moderated workflow tests.
- No path around a required owner, evidence, or go decision.
- Security and privacy review before introducing integrations or real launch data.

No demand, conversion, or operational impact is claimed by this sample.
