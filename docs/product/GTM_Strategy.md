# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Launch Control

## 1. Product Positioning
**Tagline:** Launch day, minus the heart attack.
**Elevator Pitch:** Launch Control is the ultimate mission control for software releases. It coordinates engineering deployments, QA signoffs, and marketing go-lives into a single, dependency-aware dashboard, ensuring you never launch broken features to an unaware customer base.

## 2. Target Market
- **Primary Persona:** Technical Program Managers (TPMs) and Release Managers.
- **Secondary Persona:** VP of Engineering, VP of Product Marketing.
- **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):** Late-stage startups (Series C+) and Enterprise teams who practice weekly or daily deployments and have strict compliance requirements.

## 3. Pricing Strategy
- **Base Platform:** $499/month (includes up to 50 users).
- **Enterprise Plan:** $1,500/month (SSO, Audit Logs, Custom SLA, Datadog integration).
- **Why this pricing?** We are a high-value, enterprise-grade tool. We are selling peace of mind and preventing expensive botched launches. Our pricing reflects enterprise risk mitigation.

## 4. Acquisition Channels
- **Webinars & Thought Leadership:** Host monthly panels with DevOps leaders from Netflix, Stripe, and Vercel on "How to build a culture of safe deployments."
- **Direct Enterprise Sales:** Outbound account-based marketing (ABM) targeting VP of Eng at pre-IPO companies. Send high-end physical direct mail (e.g., a "Red Button" desk toy).

## 5. Launch Timeline
- **Month 1:** Announce seed funding round in TechCrunch.
- **Month 2:** Open Early Access Program (cap at 20 companies to ensure white-glove onboarding).
- **Month 4:** General Availability (GA) Launch at AWS re:Invent or KubeCon.
